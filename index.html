About Me

The Designer

I'm Alex Chen, a product designer based in San Francisco with 5+ years of experience shipping digital products for startups and global brands alike.

My work lives at the intersection of strategic thinking and meticulous craft. Good design isn't just how something looks — it's how it works, how it feels, and how it quietly fits into someone's day.

Before going independent, I led design at a Series B startup and worked at agencies in New York and London. I've shipped products used by millions of people and I'm still chasing the feeling of getting something exactly right.

Currently open to freelance projects and select full-time opportunities.
